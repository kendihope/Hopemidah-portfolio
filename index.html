Process

  1. 01
    Talk to real users
    Interviews and surveys before pixels. The insight that matters is usually the one nobody asked for.
  2. 02
    Define the problem
    Turn scattered frustrations into one sentence worth solving.
  3. 03
    Design in high fidelity
    Real content, real hierarchy, early. It surfaces what wireframes hide.
  4. 04
    Test, then refine
    Put it in front of the people it's for, and let their reactions rewrite the interface.
